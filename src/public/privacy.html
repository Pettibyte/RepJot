REP JOT Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-16. Publisher: Pettibyte LLC.

Summary

REP JOT is a workout log. Your workout data lives in your own Google Drive account, in a private folder that only REP JOT can read. We do not run a server that stores your workouts. We do not sell your data and we show no ads.

Data REP JOT Reads From Google

When you sign in, REP JOT asks Google for two permissions:

  • Your basic Google profile: name, email address, and profile picture. REP JOT uses these to show who is signed in.
  • The Google Drive appDataFolder scope. This gives access only to the files that REP JOT creates in your Drive. It gives no access to your other Drive files, folders, or documents.

REP JOT requests no other Google permission.

Where Your Data Lives

REP JOT writes your workout log, your settings, and your program files to your Google Drive appDataFolder. Google stores those files. They are encrypted at rest by Google. REP JOT holds no copy on our own servers, because REP JOT has no storage server.

REP JOT also keeps a copy on the device you use. See "Local Browser Storage" below.

How REP JOT Uses Your Data

REP JOT uses your data for one purpose: to show you your own workout log.

  • We do not read your data for advertising.
  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking scripts.
  • We do not send your workout data to a third party.
  • No person at Pettibyte LLC reads your workouts on a normal basis.

Your browser talks to Google directly. Your data travels between your browser and Google over HTTPS. It does not pass through a REP JOT server.

Local Browser Storage

REP JOT uses local browser storage to work offline and to keep you signed in. This storage is required for the app to work. We use no storage that needs your consent.

The app stores these items on your device:

  • Sign-in state. Your Google access token and refresh token, so you do not sign in at every visit.
  • Workout cache. A copy of your Drive files in IndexedDB, so the app opens fast and works without a network.
  • In-progress workout. The set you are doing, so a reload does not lose it.
  • Device settings. Your unit preference and your display choices.

Clearing browser data removes these items. Your Drive files stay. The next sign-in downloads them again.

Export Your Data

Open Settings, then Export. You can download every REP JOT file from your Drive as plain JSON. The download needs no extra permission and leaves nothing behind on our side.

Delete Your Data

You can delete your REP JOT data at any time. Three options exist:

  • Delete from the app. Open Settings, then Danger Zone, then Delete All User Data. This deletes the REP JOT files in your Drive and clears this device.
  • Revoke access. Open your Google Account Security page, choose Third-party apps with account access, and remove REP JOT. REP JOT can no longer read your Drive.
  • Ask us. Email the address in "Contact" below. Give your Google email address and ask for deletion. We answer within 30 days.

Because your data lives in your own Drive, you can also delete the files yourself in Google Drive.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Identity Services. Handles sign-in. Google's privacy statement covers that step.
  • Google Drive API. Stores and reads your files in your own Drive account.
  • GitHub Pages. Delivers this website. GitHub logs standard web requests, such as the page address and the time.

REP JOT loads no code from a content delivery network. Every script, font, and icon comes from this site.

Security

  • The site uses HTTPS only.
  • Your tokens stay in your browser. They never reach a Pettibyte LLC server.
  • The app requests the least access Google offers for this job.

Children

REP JOT is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes To This Policy

If this policy changes, we update this page and change the effective date. A material change also gets a note on the REP JOT landing page for 30 days.

Contact

Send data-access requests, deletion requests, and privacy questions to support@pettibyte.com. Include your Google email address so we can find your request. We answer within 30 days.

Pettibyte LLC. This policy covers repjot.com only.
